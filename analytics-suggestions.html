Suggestions register

Improvements proposed to sharpen decisions and to discover missing information. Nothing here is implemented automatically — a reviewer compares suggestions side by side and picks the best path first.

Review workflow.
  • 1. PROPOSED — anyone (or an agent session) adds a suggestion with evidence and expected decision impact.
  • 2. REVIEWED — a human reviewer compares suggestions side by side on the same page and picks a path.
  • 3. ACCEPTED / REJECTED / DEFERRED — the reviewer records a decision and notes. Only ACCEPTED items may be implemented.
  • 4. IMPLEMENTED — a change is made in a later, separately claimed task; the suggestion links to the commit/PR.

Statuses below are the proposing agent's; the reviewer is the owner. Accepted items are implemented in a later, separately claimed task and linked back here.
