Flagged for Review: certainty audit

Reviewed 2026-11-03. The plan now names a best window — the two weeks between the conference championships and the Super Bowl — and labels the evidence behind every part of it. None of it is a guarantee.

What “free” can mean here. A green interval is a model gap in loaded records. It is not a verified absence of live radio programming. Confirmed start times still have estimated end times. Unknown fields remain unknown.

Executive finding

2027: Feb 1 – Feb 13 is the longest run with nothing on the radio (13 consecutive calendar days). The two anchors are official — MLB's 2027 season opens Mar 24/25 and runs to Sep 26, and Super Bowl LXI is Sun Feb 14, 2027 — while the NFL playoff round dates around it are modelled. 2028 (Jan 31 – Feb 12) and 2029 (Jan 29 – Feb 10) follow the same structure but are labelled ESTIMATION because neither season's schedule is released. Counting the Pro Bowl exhibition as well as league games shortens the clean run to six calendar days in every year.

What is still not verified: every individual kickoff time, every game's real end time, and whether each event actually airs on a receivable AM/FM signal at an Outer Sunset address. Treat the window as a strongly evidenced planning target, not a certified booking guarantee.
