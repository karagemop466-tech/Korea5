Audit

What was checked, what was wrong, and what is still unchecked.

This session's job was to review the repository line by line and confirm every published number against an official source. Three published defects were found. All three had passed every existing verifier, which is the reason they are written up here rather than quietly corrected.

What was recomputed rather than trusted

  • Home-field win rate: 232 of 453 decided games (51.21%), rebuilt from nfl/system/data/official-results.json and re-read live against the official standings page (32/32 clubs matched).
  • The 2025-26 home-field bootstrap: 95% interval 0.4702-0.5607, two-sided p = 0.6657, after the vacuous test was replaced.
  • The number of bets needed to detect a +1, +2, +3, +5 and +7.5 point edge at -110: 19,565 / 4,887 / 2,170 / 779 / 345.
  • The receiving-yards standard deviation behind the prop noise gates (22.65 yards) and the games required for a +3, +5 and +10 yard edge.
  • The two-receiver correlation r = -0.8192 on the six matched games, and its leave-one-game-out range (-0.911 to -0.780), which shows the sign does not depend on a single game.
  • All 137 completed 2026 games, across four independently built captures.
  • All 296 Week 10 injury rows, across two independently written parsers.

What this audit did not do

  • No official source was read for every one of the 1,170 games in the third-party archive; only the 2026 results were compared, because those exist in four independent official captures.
  • No claim on the site was checked against a market price, because no market price exists in the repository.
  • The official Game Book PDFs were not parsed for participation, so no replacement is proven to have played.
  • Depth charts for 31 of 32 clubs were read as committed and only Detroit's and San Francisco's were re-read live. The other 29 are as captured, and Houston's is an image and is left blank.
  • Nothing was verified about weeks 11-18 of the 2026 season: those fixtures have not been played.
