D-PRICE · Which market price will our own lines be compared against, and are we permitted to store and cite it?

open blocking Market data 11 register rows
Why it matters
Without a stored price there is no settlement, so no win rate, yield, edge or fair price can exist. This single decision blocks every monetary claim on every NFL page in this repository.
What we have
Nothing. No official NFL page publishes odds (probed by exact regex on week, game and stat pages: zero hits). One session parsed ESPN's public scoreboard odds block and labelled it a trusted secondary source, not an official feed.
What is missing
A licensed, timestamped feed of open and closing lines, plus permission to store and republish it.
How it gets closed
Obtain a licensed feed, or decide in writing to run a study-only programme with no betting conclusion.

D-SKILL · How many settled bets before a strategy may be called skill-based rather than lucky, and what correction is applied for testing many strategies?

open blocking Validation 7 register rows
Why it matters
A 55% hit rate over 40 bets is indistinguishable from a coin. Publishing one as skill is the failure mode this project exists to avoid.
What we have
A frozen gate set with explicit thresholds: minimum bets per family (30 team, 25 prop, 15 parlay), a paired sign-flip randomisation test and Benjamini-Hochberg across the whole family.
What is missing
Settled bets. Zero strategies have 100 settled bets against a stored price.
How it gets closed
Run the forward ledger until the gate's own sample requirement is met, then read the gate rather than the ROI.

D-CORR · What correlation applies between parlay legs, and should a same-game parlay be priced from the joint or from multiplied legs?

partly-answered non-blocking Markets 4 register rows
Why it matters
Multiplying legs assumes independence. Two legs on the same player are not independent, so the multiplied price is wrong in a known direction.
What we have
Measured: legs on the same player phi = 0.575 (n = 11,446); legs on different players in one game phi = 0.067 (n = 184,915); game total vs model moneyline rho = -0.047 (n = 681); margin vs total r = 0.0201 (n = 6,967).
What is missing
Prices, so the correlation can be turned into a hold figure that is quoted rather than declared.
How it gets closed
Price from the measured joint; keep the measured phi as the input and never assume independence.

D-HFA · What is the correct home-field advantage, and is the near-zero 2026 figure real or a small-sample artefact?

answered non-blocking Model 3 register rows
Why it matters
Home-field is a free parameter in every spread model; guessing it moves every line.
What we have
Official finals for 2025 and 2026: 232 of 453 decided games went to the home team (51.2%; 90% Wilson interval 47.4-55.1%; 95% bootstrap interval 47.0-56.1%; two-sided bootstrap p = 0.666). A separate model fit puts home advantage at 2.0 points.
What is missing
Nothing for a descriptive answer. It is not evidence of an edge because there is no price to beat.
How it gets closed
Answered as a description. Re-run each season.

D-REST · Which situational factors (rest, short weeks, travel) are derivable from official data, and are they signal or confound?

partly-answered non-blocking Model 4 register rows
Why it matters
Rest is the factor most often claimed as an edge and most often a proxy for opponent quality.
What we have
Exact rest days from official kickoff dates (32 short-week games, minimum 4 days rest). One lab reports rest differential as its strongest measured signal with a wide interval; the same lab's leader is not distinguishable from luck.
What is missing
A design that separates rest from opponent strength, which needs a larger sample.
How it gets closed
Pre-register a rest-only test and let the gate judge it; do not promote it on correlation alone.
