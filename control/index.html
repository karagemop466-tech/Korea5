The short answer

QuestionAnswerWhy
Can I trust a published win rate or yield here?No.No price and no settlement exist, so none is published. Every leaderboard is deliberately empty.
Is any strategy validated as skill?No.All 55 strategy rows across 6 labs are unscored or failing at least one gate. The one lab with a positive result reports it as not distinguishable from luck (t = 1.48; 95% interval spanning zero).
Do the separate NFL sections agree?Yes, on the facts that can be compared.All four independent 2026 result captures agree on all 137 completed games; the Week 10 injury report parses identically in two parsers on all 296 rows after one defect was fixed.
Does the injury/depth tracker answer the brief?Partly.It names a replacement from the club's own depth chart (verified live on Detroit: Cade Mays → Juice Scruggs, D.J. Reed → Rock Ya-Sin) but no snap count proves the replacement played.
What is the single biggest blocker?A price.D-PRICE is named by 11 separate questions across 5 registers. Until it is answered, nothing else can be graded.

How to read this site

  • Decisions — every open question, merged, with what we have, what we lack and the source that would close it.
  • Suggestions — every proposal from every register, with options, cost and a recommended default, so a path is chosen before anything is built.
  • Evidence — every dataset this site rests on, with its row count, its source and what it cannot tell you.
  • Strategies — all 55 strategy rows from all 6 labs side by side, with gate counts, so a reader can see that none passes.
  • Injuries & depth — the official weekly report by club, and the next-man-up rows the exact chart tier supports.
  • Audit — three published defects found this session, how each was found, and the check that now catches it.
  • Flagged for review — everything unverified, with the official source that would settle it. Non-blocking, by the project's own rule.

Every row links to the register or official page it came from. Regenerate with python3 tools/build_control.py && python3 tools/build_control_site.py; verify with python3 tools/verify_control.py.
