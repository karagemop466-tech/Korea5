What no source provides

  • A price. No official NFL page publishes a spread, total, moneyline or player prop. Probed by exact regex on week, game and stat pages: zero hits. This is the reason no win rate or yield is published anywhere.
  • Snap counts. No official machine-readable snap count was found, so "who replaced whom" cannot be proven from participation; the site names the chart's next man up and says so.
  • A licence for the third-party archive. Two labs use it and both publish the gap.
