NFL decision support · open questions

Decision registry — questions, information and answers

The list of questions that must be answered before any decision can reasonably be made, the information we already have, the information we do not have, and the suggestion that would discover the missing information. A decision is blocked while its missing information is unresolved — it is not guessed.

How to read this registry

  • What we know (verified) — facts confirmed against an official source, with the link.
  • What we do not have — the missing information. If it is missing, the decision is blocked.
  • Answer needed to proceed — the specific decision or value required to move forward.
  • Suggestion — the suggestion (S-xx on the Suggestions page) that would discover the missing information.

Irregularities and evidence limits (known, do not block)

  1. No market odds, prop prices, settlement, or forecast data exist in this repository. No edge, correlation value, ROI, or leaderboard result is claimed. All leaderboard rows are not qualified.
  2. No historical depth-chart or injury series exists. The 49ers depth chart is a live official snapshot (updated 11/10/26), not a history. Tier changes cannot be backfilled from injury status alone.
  3. Injury status ≠ workload. “Out/questionable/limited” does not imply a backup's expected snaps. Projected opportunity share would be a model estimate and is not asserted here.
  4. Depth-chart URL on NFL.com. nfl.com/teams/…/roster/depth-chart returned 404 in this check; the verified official tier source used here is the club's own depth-chart page (e.g. 49ers.com). Flagged for review.
  5. Rulebook URL normalization. The NFL Football Operations rulebook returned content when requested; the tool's reported canonical URL differed from the requested one. The verified landing reference is operations.nfl.com. Flagged for review.
