How to read this register

  • What we know is limited to what this repository actually holds, with an official source linked.
  • What we do not know is the specific missing information. It is not filled in or guessed.
  • Discovery step is the suggestion that would produce the missing information; it is proposed on the Suggestions page.
  • A row stays Open until the missing information is obtained or the uncertainty is explicitly accepted. Official references are starting points, not a claim that every field is available there.
