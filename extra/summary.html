What "free" means here

A minute is free when none of these is on the air:

* MLB — every game, all 30 clubs; Giants and Athletics flagged high priority.

* NFL — every game, preseason, regular season and postseason; 49ers flagged high priority. The Wild Card round window is Jan 16–18,

2027, and a Monday game inside it is modelled.

* NCAAF — Stanford and Cal, flagged high priority. Bowl games are not modelled (see below).

* Westwood One national NFL windows (Thursday, Sunday and Monday night, Thanksgiving, Christmas, playoffs, Super Bowl), and the

Pro Bowl Games.

Two scopes are shown everywhere. Radio = only events tagged Giants, Athletics, 49ers, Stanford, Cal, Westwood One or the Pro Bowl.

All games = additionally every other MLB and NFL row in the model.

Durations used for busy time: MLB 165 min (195 postseason), NFL 185 min (195 postseason), Pro Bowl 180 min, NCAAF 200 min. These are

planning estimates. The official MLB 2025 average game time is 2:38 (158 min); the rest are labelled ESTIMATION.

Building on the strategy lab, this update adds the decision-support layer the project asked for: a dedicated Suggestions section for reviewing and choosing a path before implementation, a Decision registry of questions / known information / missing information / answers needed, a leaderboard-and-rewards proposal, bet-type validation gates (single / parlay / player-prop combination), correlation-value methodology, and an injury & lineup-depth tracking design for 1st/2nd/3rd/4th string on offense and defense. The full lab is published under the NFL Lines Lab.

Current status — nothing is validated and no reward is authorized. The repository still contains no timestamped market odds, player-prop prices, settlement ledger, forecast log, or historical injury-to-performance panel. Consequently:

• No line, edge, correlation value, ROI, or leaderboard winner is claimed. The correlation table and the leaderboard are published with every value blank and marked unverified / not qualified, because there is no joint-outcome or price data to compute them from.

• The leaderboard is paper-first and gated. Entries must pass the validation gates; ranking uses predeclared out-of-sample probability-score improvement over a common baseline, never streaks, raw win rate or unadjusted ROI. Any reward waits on legal/responsible-play/privacy/anti-gaming review.

• Injury and lineup tracking is sourced, not inferred. A player's tier (2nd/3rd/4th string) is read only from a club's official depth chart; availability is read only from an official injury report / inactive list. A "lineup deficiency" requires both a sourced tier change and a sourced availability change. The effect on performance is deliberately left unmodeled until a validated panel exists.

Verified official sources (live 2026-11-13): NFL injury report, NFL transactions, NFL player statistics, NFL team statistics, NFL standings, NFL schedules, and the 49ers official depth chart (1st/2nd/3rd/4th string, updated 11/10/26). NFL Skill Lab — data-driven implementation (2026-11-13, session arena-fb9c9608; re-added after consolidation merge)

The implementation now lives at `lines-lab/index.html` (rebranded NFL Skill Lab; its first draft used the `nfl-lab/` path that the parallel model-lab session owns on `main`, so it was relocated rather than overwrite another session's work).

• **Official data layer** — NFL.com Week 10 injury report (296 player rows / 28 teams / 14 games), Week 10 schedule with the Thursday final (Commanders 24, Giants 21), Week 11 schedule; fetched 2026-11-13, transcribed with provenance (`lines-lab/data/official/`).

• **Originated lines** — walk-forward margin/pace power ratings (tuned on 2016–2019 only) price every Week 10/11 2026 game; the board publishes model spread and total against the market number with edges. Held-out spread MAE 10.18 pts vs the market's 9.76 — a baseline, not an edge, and the board says so.

• **Strategy leaderboard with validation gates** — eight strategies walked forward on held-out 2020–2025 at flat −110/ML pricing. **Zero strategies qualified**: every ROI 95% confidence interval includes zero (market-favorite baseline −2.94%, n=1,615). Failed strategies stay published; leaderboard points are paper research credit only and reward concepts stay suspended behind the CLV (G5) and prospective-trial (G6) gates.

• **Correlation values (measured)** — margin–total Pearson r +0.0201 (n=6,967), cover×over phi +0.0219, same-game-parlay multipliers 0.985×–1.011× (400,000 seeded simulations per scenario), plus two-leg −110 parlay arithmetic.

• **Injury & depth tracker** — every official report row published with its source link; per-unit availability-pressure index; recorded starting QBs with dataset start counts. Depth-chart tier (2nd/3rd string) is **not** inferred anywhere until depth-chart data lands (flags NFL-K4/NFL-K10).

• **Suggestions review board** (`lines-lab/suggestions.html`) and a **decision register** (`lines-lab/decisions.html`) — 14 proposals and 8 decision questions with have / don't-have / discovery-step columns.

• **Discovery implemented** — `.github/workflows/lines-lab-data.yml` snapshots nflverse injuries/depth-charts/snap-counts and the results/market table weekly in CI, which unblocks CLV measurement, the injury→outcome panel, depth facts and player props.
