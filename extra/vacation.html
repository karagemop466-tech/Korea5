Free vacation windows — strict verified view

A minute is free when no covered game or national/local radio broadcast is on air. A free day has no verified game with a published start, no TBA/unverified official row and no estimate-only row. Windows are ranked by free days, then by fewest unverified days.

This page is the strict, all-games lens. A window is only “fully free” here when every day in it has zero loaded rows of any kind, so nothing reaches seven days in 2027–2029: MLB plays a slate almost every day of its season. For the dated recommendation from the radio lens — Feb 1 – Feb 13, 2027, with the evidence behind each anchor — see Best vacation dates and the certainty audit.
