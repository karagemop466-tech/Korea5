Free vacation windows — strict verified view

A minute is free when no covered game or national/local radio broadcast is on air. A free day has no verified game with a published start, no TBA/unverified official row and no estimate-only row. Windows are ranked by free days, then by fewest unverified days.

Headline. In 2027 there is no fully free 7-day window: MLB schedules games on almost every day from Spring Training (Feb 19) through Sep 26, and NFL / college football fills the winter weekends. The best strict 1-week window is Jan 24–30 2027 with six verified free days and one estimate-layer day; the best 2-week and 3-week windows are Jan 24 – Feb 6 and Jan 24 – Feb 13 2027. The four days immediately after Super Bowl LXI (Feb 15–18 2027) are the cleanest verified gap before Spring Training opens Feb 19. 2028 and 2029 have no released schedules, so their windows are ESTIMATION — not a real verified released schedule.
