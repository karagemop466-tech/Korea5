The short answer. There is no fully free 7-day window in 2027, 2028 or 2029. MLB schedules a full slate almost every day from late February to early October (verified for 2027), and NFL/college football fills the winter weekends. The longest run with nothing on air is exactly six days in 2027, 2028 and 2029 — and it recurs in January and February, in the Mon–Sat gaps between NFL playoff weekends. In 2027 the six-day options are Jan 25 – Jan 30, 2027, Feb 1 – Feb 6, 2027, Feb 8 – Feb 13, 2027 — the last of which sits after the Super Bowl and before Spring Training, when nothing at all is in season. How this is built

1 · Official schedules

2026 and 2027 MLB come straight from MLB's StatsAPI (2,430 regular-season games per season, all 30 clubs). 49ers, Stanford, Cal and Westwood One are taken from their own sites.

2 · Explicit estimates

Where a league has not published a schedule (2027 NFL, all of 2028–29), the planner models the league's structure and labels every row estimated — no matchups are invented.

3 · Re-verified

A CI job re-downloads each cited official page and checks that every curated row still appears there. Failures are published on Flagged for review.
