NFL Lines Lab

A workbench for originating your own NFL lines: the questions that have to be answered first, the strategies that could be tested, the validation gates each one has to pass, the measured dependence between legs, the injury and depth tracker that feeds the signals, and a suggestions register where every improvement is listed with a recommended path. Nothing here is a validated betting edge. This repository holds no market price, no settlement record and no forecast log, so no win rate, yield, edge or fair price is published anywhere on this site.

What this lab is. A decision-support system built on official NFL records: the weekly injury report, official player game logs, and the official Game Book. What it is not. A betting service, a price feed, or a validated strategy. The leaderboard is published empty on purpose.
