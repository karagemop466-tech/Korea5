Injuries and lineup depth

Three separate official facts, kept separate on purpose: availability (the weekly injury report), who actually started and who absorbed the work (the official Game Book), and the tier label, which is this project's own model output and is only written when the Game Book supports it. Anything without that support stays blank and is listed on the flagged page.

Why the layers are not merged. An injury report says a player is doubtful. It never says who will start in his place, and it never says what that replacement is worth. A claim that a second- or third-string player replaced a starter is only supportable from a game record that names the starters and the participants — which is what the official Game Book provides.

Design of the tracker

  • Capture. Fetch the weekly injury report and the Game Book for every completed game; store each with its own retrieval timestamp and its source URL. The records behind this page are in data/lab/ in that shape.
  • Reconcile. Where two official pages disagree, publish both readings and flag the difference. Never reconcile silently.
  • Label. Apply the layer C rules to the Game Book evidence only. A tier is never inferred from an injury status.
  • Feed. The output is a per-position availability and depth table with the official source on every row, ready to be joined with a price feed when one exists.
  • Refresh. Automation of this refresh is the open suggestion P12; the records on this page were captured by hand from the official pages on 2026-11-13.
