Leaderboard

Strategies ranked by a pre-declared proper scoring rule, paper-only.

Verification rule. Every row on this site links to the official page it came from. Where an official source has not published a value the cell is left blank and the row is listed on the Flagged for Review page. Nothing is inferred and nothing is filled in.

Ranking rule

The board ranks on Out-of-sample Brier skill against a constant 0.50 forecaster. That metric was fixed before the scores were computed. It was chosen because it is a proper scoring rule - it rewards honest probabilities rather than confident ones - and because it has a natural no-skill reference: a forecaster that always says 0.50. The board is deliberately not ranked by raw win rate or by streak, both of which reward luck.

Qualification rule

An entry appears as qualified only if its paired bootstrap 90% interval excludes zero and its one-sided p-value is below 0.05. Entries that fail are still listed, with their blanks visible, so failed ideas stay in the audit history instead of disappearing.

Rewards

No rewards are authorized. The competition runs paper-only for at least one full season. Attaching money or prizes needs a governance, jurisdictional, tax and privacy review that has not happened (decision D11, suggestion SUG-12). Flagged and unqualified items do not block anything.
