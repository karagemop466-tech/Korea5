Strategy leaderboard

The competition design, and the reason its table is empty. The rules are fixed before any strategy is traded, so that the board measures skill rather than the order in which variants were tried.

Mode: paper only. No policy, legal review, funding or data licence exists yet, and no strategy has passed the gates. Real-money rewards are not authorised by this site.

Eligibility

  • All gates G0 to G8 passed and signed off.
  • Every price, forecast and settlement archived with capture timestamps.
  • Results reported out-of-sample on a locked forward period.
  • Uncertainty reported next to the score.
  • Multiplicity of the strategy family disclosed.

What is scored

Primary metric: Improvement in out-of-sample log loss against the de-vigged market probability for the same bets.

Secondary metrics: Brier score improvement, Calibration slope and intercept, Coverage of the quoted range, Observation count against the minimum evidence rule

How luck is kept out

  • Never rank by win rate alone or by streak.
  • Apply shrinkage toward the market baseline so a small sample cannot win the board.
  • Publish the observation count and the confidence interval with every rank.
  • Keep failed strategies in the audit history with the gate they failed.
  • Fix the strategy family before the trial; publish how many variants were tried.
  • Demote a strategy the moment it stops meeting the minimum evidence rule.

Rewards

Paper points computed from the primary metric, weighted by the observation count and capped so that a single lucky month cannot take the lead. Monetary rewards require a documented policy and legal review that do not exist today.

Audit rules

  • Every published rank keeps the run that produced it.
  • A demotion is published with the date and the reason.
  • The board can be rebuilt from the archived inputs alone.

Minimum evidence that each row would have to clear

Computed on the correlations page: at a -110 price a true win rate of 57.38% needs 779 resolved bets to be distinguishable from luck at 95% confidence and 80% power; 55.38% needs 2,170. A strategy claiming a smaller edge needs proportionally more bets, and the row would say so.
