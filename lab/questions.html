Decision register — what must be answered before any decision is reasonable

Fifteen decisions stand between this repository and a defensible line. For each one: what is already known, what is missing, why the missing part blocks the decision, and the exact discovery step that closes it. A question is only closed when its information exists, not when an assumption is made.

Two questions block everything. Q01 and Q05 — which prices are legally accessible, and where historical lines come from — have no answer in this repository. Until they do, no strategy can be scored, no leaderboard row can be filled, and no yield can be quoted anywhere on this site.

Q08How many observations are needed before an apparent edge can be told apart from luck?This is computed, not assumed.
