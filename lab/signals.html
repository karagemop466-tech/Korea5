Luck separation

Pythagorean expectation (exponent 2.37) estimates the win percentage a team's scoring record implies. The gap between that and the actual win percentage is the part of the standings that scoring does not explain, which is the best available proxy for luck. A positive value means the team has won more than its scoring deserves and is a regression candidate; a negative value means the opposite. This is a candidate signal (STR-05) and has not been backtested.
