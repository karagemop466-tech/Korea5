Strategy catalogue and validation gates

Twelve candidate signals: single bets, player props, prop combinations and parlays, plus the injury overlay that feeds all of them. Every one is a proposal. None has been traded, none has a price, and none may be ranked until it has passed the gates below.

Validation gates — each one must pass, in order

IDGateWhat it requiresWhy it existsWhat happens on failureEvidence in this repositoryBasis
G0Minimum evidenceA strategy may not be ranked until it has enough resolved observations to separate its claimed edge from luck at 95% confidence and 80% power. At a -110 price: 779 bets for a +5 point edge, 2,170 for +3 points, 19,565 for +1 point. A correlation of 0.3 needs about 100 games.Small samples make luck indistinguishable from skill. Publishing the minimum next to the number lets anyone check the claim.Strategy stays unpublished on the leaderboard and moves to the flagged list with its observation count.Computed from the published sample-size table on the correlations page.basis
G1Provenance and point-in-time captureEvery input and every forecast is stored immutably with the source URL, the capture timestamp and the definition used.A forecast that cannot be traced to the information available at the time cannot be tested.The record is rejected and the affected bets are excluded from scoring.Model of data/lab/*.json, each row carrying its official source URL.basis
G2Coverage and reconciliationMissing values are recorded as missing. Two official sources that disagree are published as a discrepancy, never silently reconciled.Silent fills create fake accuracy and hide the limits of the data.The affected rows are blank, flagged, and listed on the Flagged for Review page.Example: the Washington and New York records printed differently on two official pages; both are published as printed.basis
G3BaselineA strategy must be compared with a de-vigged market reference captured at the same decision cut-off. A model that cannot beat the market on the same bets has no claim.Beating a coin flip is not the standard; beating the price is.No strategy may be scored for edge until the reference series exists.Blocked on Q01 and Q05; no quote data exists in this repository.basis
G4Leakage audit and time-ordered splitsFitting happens inside training folds only, on data strictly earlier than the prediction. All transformations are fitted inside the fold.Leakage produces beautiful backtests and no live performance.The run is discarded and repeated from a clean split.Rule published here; no backtest has been run.basis
G5Out-of-sample scoring with uncertaintyScore on data never used in fitting, with a proper scoring rule and a calibration check. Report the confidence interval. Report yield only where actual prices and actual settlements were captured.A win rate without an interval is a story, not a result.No published performance claim; the strategy is flagged as unproven.Rule published here; no performance claim is made anywhere on this site.basis
G6Robustness and multiple testingCheck vig, pushes, voids, stale prices and rest rules; control for the number of strategies tried, since a family of tests produces false winners by chance; confirm the result holds across seasons and defined subgroups.Testing many variants and publishing the best one is how luck is mistaken for skill.Strategy is demoted and the multiplicity is published alongside it.Rule published here; the number of strategies in each family is recorded when it happens.basis
G7Locked prospective trialAfter passing G1 to G6 on history, freeze the strategy and run it forward on live bets for a pre-declared horizon with no parameter changes.The only clean test of a model is the future it has not seen.The strategy returns to development and keeps its history.Rule published here; no trial has started.basis
G8Independent review and audit historyA second reviewer signs off before a strategy is ranked. Failed strategies stay in the public history with the reason they failed.A leaderboard that deletes its failures cannot be audited.Ranking is withheld.Rule published here; no strategy has been reviewed.basis
Gate G3 is currently unpassable. A strategy must beat the market on the same bets, and there is no market reference in this repository yet. That is not a defect in the strategy; it is the missing artefact recorded as Q05, and it is the critical path for the whole line-making track.
