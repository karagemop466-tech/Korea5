Competition

Unit: 1 unit = 1% of a notional 100-unit bankroll (paper units; owner may set real budget — Q11)

Windows: Weekly leaderboard (Week 10 onward) · Season leaderboard 2026-27 · Multi-season rolling (from 2027)

Eligibility

  • Strategy registered with a unique ID and pre-kickoff lock (G1)
  • All inputs from verified rows or labelled MODEL OUTPUT (G2)
  • Published parameters (G3)
  • Exposure caps respected (G6)
  • Multi-leg tickets declare correlation assumptions (G7)

Scoring — skill, not ROI

rawROI = net units / staked
shrunkROI = (n·ROI + 25·0)/(n+25)
skillScore = 100·(0.6·z(shrunkROI) + 0.4·z(CLV))

Tie-breaks: higher shrunkROI → larger n → lower exposure used → earlier registration. any rule-violation week voids that week from scoring.

Anti-luck rules

  • Rewards require 95% bootstrap CI of ROI to clear zero (G5)
  • Parlays count as their leg count for sample size
  • One lucky outlier week is winsorized at the 99th percentile of weekly ROI before scoring
  • Rank display shows n and CI width beside every strategy so readers see precision
