Honesty policy. Every fact row links to its source. Market lines (spreads/totals/moneylines) are bookmaker quotes from the open-source nflverse/nfldata dataset — labelled market, not official NFL data. Official data comes from NFL.com pages fetched 2026-11-13 (link on each row). Values that could not be verified are left blank and listed on Flagged for Review. No betting edge is claimed anywhere; the leaderboard is a paper research competition.
