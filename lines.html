Method in one paragraph

Each team gets a rating r from the game margins of Weeks 1–9 (Week 10 held out) by least squares: margin ≈ rhome − raway + HFA. Expected points use scoring/allowing averages. Injury weights move the spread only. Totals are not injury-adjusted in v0 (flagged limitation).

Parameters: HFA fitted = -0.5 pts (traditional prior ≈ 2.5 — deviation flagged); position weights and designation factors are published below.
