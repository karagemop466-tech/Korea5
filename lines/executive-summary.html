Model outputs, not official values, and not betting advice. Every number on these pages is computed from official NFL.com results, official box scores and official club depth charts. The originated lines, probabilities, returns and rewards are this repository's model output - they are not published by the league and they are not a market price. The reference price of -110 per leg is a declared convention used so strategies can be compared with each other. No claim is made that any strategy beats a real market. See Validation and Questions.

Executive summary

The short version of what was built, what it found, and what it cannot answer.

What was built

  • A line origination model that produces its own spread and total for every NFL regular-season game from 2024 to the latest completed week of 2026, from official NFL.com results only, walk-forward so a game is never predicted with information from its own result.
  • A player-prop projection engine over official box scores: recency-weighted production adjusted for the opponent's allowance rate, benchmarked against the player's own prior average.
  • Eighteen strategies across team selections, player props and parlays, each priced against a declared -110 reference price, each scored against a pre-registered naive baseline on identical events.
  • Seven validation gates applied in order, a reward formula, a skill leaderboard, a measured correlation matrix, and an injury-and-depth tracker that names the replacement tier when a starter is unavailable.
  • Published registers for suggestions to review before implementation, and for the questions that must be answered before a decision can reasonably be made.

What it cannot answer

  • Whether any of this beats a real market. No official source publishes betting prices, so every price here is a declared convention.
  • Whether injury information adds value. The league publishes only the current week's report, so injury signals cannot be backtested at all yet.
  • Whether a depth chart is right. The chart is the club's own document and several clubs label theirs unofficial.
  • How a prop bet would actually settle. Settlement rules are bookmaker rules.
  • Whether the leaderboard should reward anything other than paper points. That is a governance decision that has not been taken.

The decision this section is built to support

The register on the questions page lists what must be answered before a decision can reasonably be made, with a concrete discovery step for each gap. The suggestions page lists the changes that could then be made, each with the decision it unblocks and the risk of getting it wrong. Both are meant to be reviewed before any further build happens, and both are published rather than assumed.
