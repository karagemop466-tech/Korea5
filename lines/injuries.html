Injury and depth tracker

For every game in the published schedule, this tracker joins the official NFL.com weekly injury report to the club's own official depth chart. Where a starter is unavailable it names the tier that replaces them, on both sides of the ball.

How to read this. The injury report says a player is Out, Doubtful or Questionable. The depth chart says who is behind him. A row where a first-string starter is unavailable and a second or third string replaces him is the kind of deficiency worth reviewing before a line moves. The tracker reports the official facts; it does not estimate how much the line should move — no official source supports that.
