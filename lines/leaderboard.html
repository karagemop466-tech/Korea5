Model outputs, not official values, and not betting advice. Every number on these pages is computed from official NFL.com results, official box scores and official club depth charts. The originated lines, probabilities, returns and rewards are this repository's model output - they are not published by the league and they are not a market price. The reference price of -110 per leg is a declared convention used so strategies can be compared with each other. No claim is made that any strategy beats a real market. See Validation and Questions.

Leaderboard

Strategies are ranked by the reward they earn under the published formula. A strategy earns nothing unless it passes all seven gates, so a large return on a thin sample earns zero.

Reward formula (published). reward = 1000 × skill × evidence × luck guard, where skill is the relative log-loss improvement over the pre-registered naive baseline on the same events, evidence is min(1, bets / 50), and luck guard falls to zero when a single bet carries more than 40% of gross profit. The scale is 0–1000 points. These are paper points. No money, no wagering and no sportsbook price is involved — see QN-12.

What is deliberately not here

  • No money leaderboard. Reward is a paper score until the governance questions are answered.
  • No ranking by win rate or streak. Win rate alone cannot separate skill from luck.
  • No strategy that failed a gate has a reward, however large its return looks.
