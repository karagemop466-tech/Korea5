Model outputs, not official values, and not betting advice. Every number on these pages is computed from official NFL.com results, official box scores and official club depth charts. The originated lines, probabilities, returns and rewards are this repository's model output - they are not published by the league and they are not a market price. The reference price of -110 per leg is a declared convention used so strategies can be compared with each other. No claim is made that any strategy beats a real market. See Validation and Questions.

Proposals to review before anything is changed. Each suggestion states the decision it unblocks, the effort, the risk of getting it wrong, and the official evidence behind it. Nothing here is implemented unless a separate page shows the built result.

How to use this page. Read the suggestion and its evidence, then mark it ACCEPT, DECLINE or NEED MORE INFO in the Status column when you decide. Only an accepted suggestion becomes a build task. The Status column is the decision record — it is not updated by the site.
