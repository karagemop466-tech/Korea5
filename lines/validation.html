Model outputs, not official values, and not betting advice. Every number on these pages is computed from official NFL.com results, official box scores and official club depth charts. The originated lines, probabilities, returns and rewards are this repository's model output - they are not published by the league and they are not a market price. The reference price of -110 per leg is a declared convention used so strategies can be compared with each other. No claim is made that any strategy beats a real market. See Validation and Questions.

Validation gates and method

A strategy is only allowed to compete if it passes every gate below. The gates exist to separate repeatable skill from a lucky stretch, and they are applied in order.

The seven gates

GateRequirementOfficial source
G1Provenance - every input carries an official source URL (checked by scripts/lines/verify_lines.py)NFL.com official results
G2Evidence - minimum number of bets placed after the price filterNFL.com official results
G3Baseline - beats the pre-registered naive model out of sample (log loss lower than the baseline)NFL.com official results
G4Significance - bootstrap 95% CI on ROI lies entirely above zero AND a paired sign-flip randomisation test shows the strategy beats its baseline (Benjamini-Hochberg adjusted p < 0.05 across the whole family)NFL.com official results
G5Holdout - positive ROI on the untouched 2026 regular seasonNFL.com official results
G6Robustness - positive ROI in at least two seasons and no single bet carries more than 40% of gross profitNFL.com official results
G7Non-redundancy - |correlation| below 0.50 with every higher-ranked qualified strategyNFL.com official results

How the test is set up

Design choiceWhat was doneWhy it mattersOfficial source
Time-ordered splitsThe 2024 and 2025 regular seasons are the development period; the 2026 regular season is the untouched holdout.A holdout that was ever looked at is no longer a holdout.NFL.com schedule
Walk-forward inside a seasonEvery game is predicted only from games played strictly earlier. No statistic used for a game is computed from that game.Removes hindsight leakage from the model itself.NFL.com official results
Pre-registered baselineEach strategy is compared with a naive model — league-average total, pick'em, or the player's own prior average — on the identical events.A strategy only earns credit for adding something to a rule a reader could write themselves.NFL.com official box scores
Declared reference price-110 per leg, compounded for parlays. A strategy only places a bet when its own probability exceeds the price-implied probability.Selection is part of the strategy. It also means no claim is made about any real market.NFL.com official game pages
Uncertainty, not point estimatesBootstrap confidence intervals on return, a paired sign-flip randomisation test on the skill a strategy adds over its baseline, Benjamini-Hochberg correction across the whole family, calibration error and Brier score all published.With 18 strategies tested, one lucky result is expected. Correction for that is the difference between a finding and a coincidence.NFL.com official results
Luck controlGate G6 refuses a strategy whose gross profit is concentrated in a single bet, and requires a positive result in at least two seasons.Removes the most common way a backtest is fooling itself.NFL.com official schedule by season
Redundancy controlGate G7 compares each qualified strategy with every better-ranked qualified strategy and refuses to pay twice for the same signal.Stops a leaderboard from paying one idea three times.NFL.com official results

What this method cannot tell you

  • It cannot tell you whether a strategy beats a real market, because no official market price exists to compare against. See QN-01 and FL-05.
  • It cannot tell you whether injury information adds value, because the league publishes only the current week's injury report. See FL-03.
  • It cannot tell you whether a depth chart is right, because the chart belongs to the club and several clubs label theirs unofficial. See FL-04 and FL-08.
  • It cannot tell you how a prop bet would actually settle, because settlement rules are bookmaker rules. See FL-09.
