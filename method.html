Method, definitions and assumptions

This page exists so every number on the site can be traced to a rule. If a rule here looks wrong to you, the calendar is wrong in a predictable way — tell me and it can be changed in one place.

What “free” means

A minute is free when none of the following is on air:

  • MLB — all 30 clubs, Spring Training through the World Series, including the All-Star Game (Giants and Athletics flagged high priority).
  • NFL — every game, preseason through the Super Bowl (49ers flagged high priority).
  • NCAAF — Stanford and California (high priority).
  • Westwood One Sports — national NFL radio broadcasts.

Three scopes are available on the calendar: All games (the list above), Radio only (games documented as airing on AM/FM in San Francisco) and High priority (Giants, Athletics, 49ers, Stanford, Cal, Westwood One).

Busy time is the scheduled start plus the average length of that kind of game. These are planning durations, not guarantees.

Time zone

Everything is computed and displayed in Pacific time (America/Los_Angeles). Westwood One publishes Eastern time; those rows are converted at ET−3h, which is correct for PST dates (from 2026-11-01). Daylight-saving boundaries are handled by the IANA time zone database, not by a fixed offset.

Estimates vs verified data

Every row carries a status:

  • verified — an official source published the date, the matchup and the start time.
  • estimated — either the date is official but the start time is TBD (most of the 2027 MLB season), or the whole row is a modelled league window because the season has not been released.
  • unconfirmed — the date is known but a value is missing (for example the 49ers' Week 18 date).

How estimated start times are produced

  1. 2027 MLB. MLB's feed carries a placeholder time for 1,631 regular-season games. For those, the planner takes the most common Pacific start time that the home club actually used on that weekday in the released 2026 season. If the club has no such history, the league-wide modal time for that weekday is used.
  2. 2027–2029 NFL. Kickoff is the Thursday after Labor Day; 18 weeks; fixed Sunday/Thursday/Monday windows; Thanksgiving on the fourth Thursday; Super Bowl on the second Sunday of February.
  3. 2027–2029 NCAAF. Twelve Saturdays per school, late August to late November, blocked at 12:30 PM and 4:30 PM Pacific because television sets kickoff late. This deliberately over-blocks rather than pretending to know the slot.
  4. 2028–2029 MLB. Opening Day is the last Thursday of March, a daily slate runs to the last Sunday of September, the All-Star Game is the second Tuesday of July, and the postseason starts the following Tuesday. Slate days are modelled as one row per day with no invented matchups.

What this model does not do

  • It does not include rain delays, postponements, flex scheduling or overtime.
  • It does not include pre-game or post-game radio shows (typically 30–60 minutes either side).
  • It does not include other Westwood One properties (NCAA Basketball, golf, soccer) beyond the one published NCAA Football window.
  • It does not claim a station for the Athletics or for the Westwood One feed in San Francisco — those are flagged.
