Executive summary — NFL intelligence

2026-11-13 · mirrors research/EXECUTIVE-SUMMARY-NFL.md

The short version

We now originate our own NFL numbers from verified league data. The 2026 season to date is fully collected and cross-checked: 137 final scores (weeks 1–10) reproduce the official standings team-by-team, 32 of 32, exactly — records and points. From those results we compute team ratings and publish our own spreads, totals and win probabilities for every remaining Week-10 game and all of Week 11. A walk-forward backtest (weeks 5–9) shows real, if modest, skill: both core strategies beat naive baselines on spread error and probability error and pass the four evaluative validation gates.

What was delivered

  • Lines origination: opponent-adjusted margin model + recency variant; formulas published; market columns deliberately blank (no verified odds source — Q-ODDS).
  • Signals: rest-days difference is the strongest measured pre-game signal (r=0.438 vs margin), ahead of rating difference (0.325). This is a computed finding from verified data, not a claim from literature.
  • Strategy system: six strategies (singles, parlays, player-prop combos) with eight validation gates that remove luck: sample floors, baseline-relative skill, out-of-time holdouts, multiple-testing correction, bootstrap certainty and closing-line grading.
  • Leaderboard competition: pre-registered entries, Brier/log-loss skill scoring, non-monetary rewards pending a legal answer (Q-LEGAL), pilot board live for weeks 10–18.
  • Injury & deficiency tracking: the official Week-10 report (296 rows) plus verified starter/backup news. Confirmed deficiency chains: Baltimore QB (starter on IR and backup out for season — 3rd/4th string), Seattle QB (starter out, named backup to start), Miami QB (starter IR, backup first start), Green Bay RB, Rams WR.
  • Decision support: a suggestions register with a review-and-choose workflow (nothing implemented before acceptance) and a questions register listing exactly what is missing and how to discover it.

What blocks bigger decisions (read before acting)

  • No market odds source exists in this system (Q-ODDS) — monetary grading, CLV and realistic parlay pricing cannot start until one is licensed or logged. This is the #1 blocker.
  • Depth tiers are unverified except where official news names them (Q-DEPTH) — 2nd/3rd/4th-string claims are labelled only for the verified chains above.
  • Only one week of injury history exists in this repo (Q-INJ-HIST); the injury signal cannot be correlated with outcomes yet.
  • Rewards for external entries need a compliance answer (Q-LEGAL) before the 2027 season opens.
