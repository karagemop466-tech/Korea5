Injury Tracking & Depth-Chart Deficiencies

Track injuries and identify defensive and offensive deficiencies when a starter is replaced by a 2nd, 3rd or 4th-string backup. These adjustments feed the originating-line signals.

Designation meanings (per the NFL): Out = will not play; Doubtful = unlikely to play; Questionable = uncertain to play. Practice status shows whether a player took full, limited or no reps during the week.

Depth-chart deficiency register

When a starter is out, the replacement level (2nd / 3rd / 4th string) is the deficiency input. The NFL does not publish an official depth-chart replacement feed, so all replacement cells are blank and flagged unverified.

How injuries feed the line

Starter out

If a starter carries an "Out" designation, the replacement's expected performance replaces the starter's in the originating line model. The replacement depth (2nd/3rd/4th string) is logged here.

Limited practice

"Limited Participation" suggests reduced readiness; flag as a watch item until the player is upgraded to Full or downgraded to Out/Doubtful.

Gate 5

Validation Gate 5 requires injury/depth inputs to be populated or flagged before a strategy is leaderboard-eligible (see Strategies).
