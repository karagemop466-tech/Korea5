Entry rules

  • Each strategy is submitted as frozen code + a written rule card before its first graded pick (pre-registration prevents result-shopping).
  • Pick types allowed: single bets, parlays (2-5 legs), player-prop combos (2-4 legs). Mixed-type strategies are scored per type and overall.
  • Picks are graded against recorded lines at submission time (market lines required — see Q-ODDS; until a verified odds source exists, forecast scoring uses Brier/log-loss against outcomes only).
  • No entry may change after kickoff of its first graded pick; changes create a new entry.

Rewards (non-monetary until Q-LEGAL is answered)

  • Tiered research credits: access to deeper verified datasets (gamebooks, snap counts) as they are added
  • Named badge on the leaderboard page and in the executive summary (no monetary prize is offered — see Q-LEGAL)
  • Winning rule cards are promoted into the maintained strategy library with author credit

How luck is removed

  • Minimum sample sizes (G1) kill lucky streaks
  • Baseline-relative gates (G2/G3) kill coin flips dressed as models
  • Out-of-time holdout (G8) kills overfitting
  • Deflated Sharpe / multiple-testing correction (G7) kills best-of-many luck
  • Bootstrap confidence intervals (G6) kill small-edge illusions
  • Closing-line comparison (G5) kills stale-price luck

Submissions, gate results and re-scoring are logged on this page and in COORDINATION.md. Unverified claims never enter the board.

Conservative joint-probability bounds are defined in the frameworks below.

Parlay framework

  • max 5 legs, min 2
  • no two legs may be the same event
  • same-game legs carry a published correlation penalty before joint probability is computed
  • Correlation policy: Joint probability bounds: lower bound max(0, sum p_i - (k-1)), upper bound min(p_i). Empirical correlations are published in correlationValues when at least 60 graded pairs exist; until then combos use the conservative lower bound.
  • Sizing: quarter-Kelly on the joint edge, capped at 2% of bankroll per ticket
