Executive summary

Question: can we set our own NFL lines and find strategies that beat the market, with the luck taken out? Answer as of this build: not yet, and the evidence points the other way.

What we found

  1. Our line model is less accurate than the market. Walk-forward on 2016–2025 (K chosen on 2006–2015 only), the model misses the final margin by 10.17 points on average. The market spread misses by 9.79. Our model is a useful benchmark, not a better price.
  2. No strategy passes every gate. 10 strategies were tested against six gates. None passes all six. The best by lower confidence bound is MODEL-EDGE-3: 783 bets, hit rate 52.2% (95% CI 50.0% to 54.6%), p = 0.2243. That is consistent with luck.
  3. The market inputs are not verified. The spread and total lines come from a community dataset whose documentation does not name the source. Gate G0 therefore fails for every strategy. Results are provisional until a documented source is confirmed.
  4. Injury-based signals are too thin to test. Only 7 games from 2016 to 2025 had a starting QB listed Out or Doubtful on the official report (and the QB-based strategies are therefore untestable).
  5. Correlation is real, and parlays do not rescue the edge. Edge strategies overlap heavily (see Correlations). For two-leg parlays, the joint win rate is 28.8% for the top pair versus 27.1% if the legs were independent, so the naive independence estimate understates the hit rate for these pairs. But the top pair is two nested versions of one edge (game overlap 100%), and 0 of 10 parlays pass all five parlay gates.
  6. Current injury picture (Week 10, official report). 8 players are listed Out. Two starters are out in a depth slot that has a named backup: DET LCB D.J. Reed (backup Khalil Dorsey) and NE LCB Carlton Davis III (backup Charles Woods). Depth data is third-party, so treat these as leads to check against the team's own depth chart.
  7. Player-prop combinations cannot be tested. No official source publishes historical prop prices.
  8. Singapore: Singapore Pools is the only licensed remote gambling operator. Taking part in unlicensed remote gambling is an offence (up to S$10,000 and/or 6 months). We cannot recommend any wager. The site is for analysis only. GRA: Unlawful remote gambling

Method in one paragraph

Team power ratings are updated after each game by 0.06 × (actual margin − predicted margin), regressed toward zero each season, with a 2.0-point home edge (none at neutral sites). Totals use an exponentially weighted average of recent game totals (α = 0.15). Every prediction uses only games played before kickoff. Strategies are scored at −110 (break-even 52.38%). Pushes are excluded.

Scope note: the analysis covers regular-season games. Playoff games are excluded from evaluation.
