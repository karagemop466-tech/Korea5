What can be decided now?

Only the build order and evidence standard can be recommended from current information: first select licensed historical market data and define market rules; then create a point-in-time paper single-bet baseline; then add injury/depth-chart features, player props and joint parlay estimates behind separate validation gates. A live price, projected edge, injury-driven adjustment, leaderboard rank and prize remain undecidable today.
