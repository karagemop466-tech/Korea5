Two labs, one question. This section builds and back-tests a line model from third-party game data. The companion NFL Lines Lab holds the evidence side of the same question, built only on official NFL records: the published injury report, official player game logs and the official Game Book, with the measured correlations, dispersion figures and minimum-sample rules computed from them. Read them together — neither one publishes a validated edge on its own.
