Executive summary

This lab turns the repository's NFL coverage into a decision system: it originates its own game lines from results-based power ratings, publishes them against the market number, runs candidate betting strategies through pre-registered validation gates on held-out seasons, measures the correlation values needed to price parlays honestly, and tracks injuries/availability from the official NFL report.

What the data says so far (2020–2025 held-out walk-forward, flat -110 pricing):
  • No strategy qualified. Every candidate's ROI 95% CI includes zero. The market-favorite baseline is −2.94% ROI (n=1,615). This is the luck-removal step working: nothing is promoted on noise.
  • The model is a baseline, not an edge. Model spread MAE 10.18 pts vs market 9.76 pts on the same window. No predictive edge over the closing line is claimed or implied.
  • Correlation is small but real to measure. Margin–total r = +0.0201; same-game-parlay multipliers measured at 0.985×–1.011× — near-independence on this dataset.
  • Availability pressure is measurable now. 14 of 28 Week 10 teams carry an elevated/critical unit from the official report. Depth-chart tier (2nd/3rd string) is deliberately not inferred — see flag NFL-K4.
  • Rewards are suspended by design until gates G5 (closing-line value) and G6 (prospective trial) are satisfiable. Paper points only.
