Executive summary

What decision-makers need to know

Recommended path

Start with auditable single-bet forecasts

Define a pregame snapshot, build calibrated probabilities for market outcomes, compare them with de-vigged market prices, and paper-track every eligible signal before any stakes or rewards.

Design proposal; no performance claim. Inputs: official NFL stats and schedules, plus a separately licensed odds archive (not present).

Critical data gap

Odds and props history is missing

Without line + price snapshots, timestamp, bookmaker/market rules, and graded outcomes, closing-line value, expected value, ROI, and parlay settlement cannot be independently evaluated.

No odds source is currently configured in this repository.

Lineup model required

Injury status is not a depth chart

The NFL injury report reports practice participation and game status. It does not itself identify exact replacement string, expected snaps, role, or tactical impact. Capture official depth charts and confirm actual participation separately.

Executive recommendation

Build a paper-only, point-in-time, single-bet probability model first. Freeze its inputs and version, compare to a timestamp-matched market baseline, and establish prospective validation criteria before adding lineups, parlays, player props, or rewards. Treat injuries as a measured lineup change only when official availability and participation evidence supports it. Do not call a strategy “skill-based” until it beats a predeclared benchmark out of sample with uncertainty reported.
