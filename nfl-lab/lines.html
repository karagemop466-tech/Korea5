Honesty policy. Every fact row links to its source. Market lines (spreads/totals/moneylines) are bookmaker quotes from the open-source nflverse/nfldata dataset — labelled market, not official NFL data. Official data comes from NFL.com pages fetched 2026-11-13 (link on each row). Values that could not be verified are left blank and listed on Flagged for Review. No betting edge is claimed anywhere; the leaderboard is a paper research competition.

How the lines are originated

Power ratings are updated game-by-game in chronological order (margin response, capped at ±18, learning rate 0.1, season regression 0.55, home advantage 2.07 pts). Totals come from pace ratings (offense scored / defense allowed). Every prediction is emitted before the ratings absorb that game — no leakage. Parameters were tuned only on 2016–2019; 2020–2025 stayed untouched for the leaderboard.

Spread convention: all spreads are in home-team coordinates (positive = home favored). Market spreads are the bookmaker consensus from nfldata games.csv (market) — labelled market data, not official NFL data. Official Week 10/11 matchups, kickoffs and results are from NFL.com (Week 10 ↗, Week 11 ↗); the 14 Week 10 and 13 Week 11 matchups on those pages match the dataset exactly (see Repo review).
