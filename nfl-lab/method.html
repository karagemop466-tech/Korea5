Model specification

A deliberately simple, fully transparent baseline: pairwise margin ratings with exponential response, plus per-team pace ratings for totals. Its purpose is to make the process real — walk-forward predictions, market comparison, gates — not to win.

Provenance & leakage rules

  • Every dataset has a provenance row (Sources): URL, fetch method, commit hash, timestamp.
  • Predictions are emitted before the observation that would update the model (code audit = gate G4).
  • Market data is labelled market everywhere; official data links to its NFL.com page on every row.
  • Missing values stay blank and are published on Flagged for Review — nothing is inferred.
  • Simulations use a fixed RNG seed (2026) so every number on this site is reproducible.
