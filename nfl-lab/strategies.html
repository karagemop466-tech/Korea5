Strategy definitions

  • MKT-HOME / MKT-FAV / MKT-DOG: baselines. Bet the home team, the favourite or the underdog against the spread every game.
  • MODEL-EDGE-n: bet the side our model prefers by at least n points against the spread.
  • REST-GAP-3: bet the team with at least 3 more days of rest.
  • QB-OUT-FADE / QB-OUT-BACK: starting QB listed Out or Doubtful on the NFL report (exactly one side). Fade = bet the other team. Back = bet the team with the injured QB. Only 7 games qualify in 2016–2025, so these are untestable.
  • TOTAL-EDGE-3: bet over/under when our total differs from the total line by 3 or more.
  • Parlays: two single-bet legs on different games in the same week. Gates P1–P5 are listed on the Correlations page.
