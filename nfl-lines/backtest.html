What is not in this backtest, and why that matters

  • No injuries. The archive's injury data is a five-column mirror with no practice status, and no official depth chart could be retrieved. Adding a fake injury feature would be worse than adding none.
  • No weather. Indoor/outdoor is known; conditions are not.
  • No player props. There are no prop prices, so a prop backtest is impossible rather than merely absent.
  • No line movement. Only one price per game exists, so no open-to-close analysis is possible.

Each of those is a flagged item, not a silent gap.
