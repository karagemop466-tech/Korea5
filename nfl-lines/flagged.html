How a blank is produced

When a source returns nothing for a field, the pipeline writes null. The site renders the literal text — blank —. No value is ever inferred, averaged in from another site, or filled from a previous season. Where a model needs a number and the value is missing, the fill is documented on the lines page and counted in the coverage table.

Contradiction policy

If two publishers disagree on a score, both values are shown and the disagreement is counted. If a source disappears (404), the field becomes blank and the row is flagged — the previous value is not carried forward silently.

NFL Lines Lab — every published number is generated from data fetched and hashed by the repository's own pipeline. No betting return, payout or edge is claimed anywhere: the only market data available is a single un-timestamped line snapshot, and no player-prop market exists in any reachable source.
