This lab builds NFL win probabilities from cited public data, tests them walk-forward, deflates the score against a best-of-N luck threshold, measures the correlations instead of assuming them, and ranks singles, parlays and player-prop combos only on what survives. Nothing here is a recommendation to bet, and no profit figure appears anywhere, because no timestamped price history exists in any reachable source.
