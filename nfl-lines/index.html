Can I originate my own NFL lines?

Yes — and this site does it, then measures whether the number is any good. The short answer: the fair lines are generated and published for 2026 Week 11, the strategies are backtested on 1999–2026, and not one of them has demonstrated skill rather than luck. That is the finding, not a failure of the build.

Signal quality: the model versus the market

The single most important number on this site. Signal is 1 − (Brier score ÷ the Brier score of always predicting the base rate). Zero means no information; higher is better. Both are scored on the same games.

Does a backup actually cost points?

Measured on 414 club-weeks: a missing tier-1 offensive player changed scoring by -0.97 points, with an interval that includes zero.

Translation: on this evidence, tier-1 absences do not move the score measurably. The quarterback-only estimate is larger but has only 18 club-weeks behind it, so it gets no interval and no betting role.
