Injuries and lineup depth

Two different questions, kept apart on purpose. Injury status is the official weekly designation (Out / Doubtful / Questionable) and is verified. Lineup depth is a separate published artefact — the depth chart — and is shown with its snapshot date because a chart from three weeks ago is not evidence about tonight.

What the numbers do and do not mean. "Starter value" is the share of the unit's snap-weighted usage held by players listed first on the depth chart. It measures usage, not talent. A missing star with low usage barely moves it. That limitation is registered as Q6 and is not papered over.

The 2nd, 3rd and 4th string problem

The depth tracker answers, per team and position: who is listed first, second, third, fourth, and is the first-string player on the report? Three states are possible and they are published separately:

Starter available

Listed 1st on the latest snapshot and not marked Out. No adjustment is made — the model already rates the team.

Starter out, backup promoted

Listed 1st but marked Out. The snap-weighted value of that absence enters the model as an Out share, and the next man up is shown by tier.

Unknown

No depth row or no report row. Left blank. The model uses the documented neutral fill, and the coverage table on the lines page shows how often that happens.

Known gaps in this tracker

  • The inactive list at kickoff is not in this dataset. A Questionable player may play; an Out player never does. Q5 covers obtaining the official inactives.
  • Depth-chart snapshots have a timestamp, but staleness is not yet enforced — Q9 proposes a 7-day rule.
  • The tracker covers the latest week only for the live board. Historical index values are used in the model for every evaluated game.

NFL Lines Lab — every published number is generated from data fetched and hashed by the repository's own pipeline. No betting return, payout or edge is claimed anywhere: the only market data available is a single un-timestamped line snapshot, and no player-prop market exists in any reachable source.
