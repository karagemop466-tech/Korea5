How to read the interval. Whole weeks are resampled with replacement, because games inside a week share conditions. If the interval includes zero, the result is consistent with a bettor who has no edge at all. Point estimates without intervals are not published anywhere on this site.

How to read "p vs market". Each bet's true win probability is set to the de-vigged closing market probability of the side taken, then 10,000 seeded simulations ask how often a bettor with no information beyond the market would earn at least this ROI. This is the sharpest honest null. Note what it does to the best-looking row: the three-leg parlay's p falls from a naive 0.0005 to 0.218 once mixed prices are handled correctly — the naive test was measuring price variance, not skill.

Gate-by-gate detail

Dome / closed-roof over

Bet the over in every game played under a dome or closed roof at the quoted price.

Inputs: Roof field from the archive (dome/closed vs outdoors/open).

Limits: Population bet; no interactive filter, so the multiple-testing risk is limited to the family size.

  • PASS provenance — every input dataset is in nfl-lines/data/sources.json
  • PASS coverage — 1436 priced bets graded
  • PASS baseline — market reference is the de-vigged closing price, not a naive guess
  • PASS leakage — walk-forward: all inputs dated before kickoff
  • PASS out — 1436 graded bets vs pre-declared minimum 300
  • FAIL robustness — 9 of 21 seasons positive
  • FAIL prospective — requires a frozen version and predictions logged before outcomes; no trial has been run, so this gate is always open
  • FAIL release — requires human review; not automated

Own fair total from point-in-time scoring rates

Build each club's points-scored and points-allowed rate with a 6-game prior toward the league mean, combine the two clubs into a fair total, and bet only when the fair total differs from the closing number by at least 1.5 points.

Inputs: Only graded results enter the rate table; the accumulator is updated after each bet is priced.

Limits: The fair total is compared with the closing line, not the opening line, so no line-move edge is claimed. Pushes are dropped.

  • PASS provenance — every input dataset is in nfl-lines/data/sources.json

Ratings (Elo) spread value

Fit the Elo probability gap to an expected home margin with an expanding least-squares regression on already-graded games, compare that fair number with the closing line, and bet when the two differ by at least 1 point.

Inputs: Same Elo as s1 plus a two-parameter margin mapping refitted point-in-time. No injury, rest or weather input.

Limits: Pushes are dropped. The mapping has to be refitted with at least 300 graded games before the strategy can place a bet, which is why the early seasons contribute no bets.

Two-leg cross-game parlay (top-2 weekly Elo edges)

Every week, take the two largest Elo moneyline edges and combine them into one two-leg cross-game parlay at the product of the two quoted moneylines.

What would have to happen for a reward to be paid

  1. The strategy passes data provenance, coverage, baseline, leakage, out-of-sample sample size and robustness.
  2. It is frozen — code, inputs and thresholds — and its predictions are logged before outcomes.
  3. The frozen trial completes a pre-declared horizon.
  4. A human review confirms there is no integrity or responsible-use problem.

Only then does the ELIGIBLE badge appear. No amount of backtest ROI substitutes for that sequence.
