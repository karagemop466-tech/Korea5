The questions that block everything else

  1. Is the model better than the market? No. Until it is, a line is a benchmark, not an alternative.
  2. Is there a legal, timestamped price archive? Without one, props and same-game parlays can never be scored.
  3. Is official depth order obtainable? Without it, backup tiers stay a computed estimate.
