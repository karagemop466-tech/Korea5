How to read this

  • Two correlated inputs are one input. If rest differential and short-week flags move together, adding both inflates the model without adding information.
  • Correlation is not edge. A signal can correlate with the outcome and still be fully priced by the market — which cannot be tested until a line history exists (Q11).

NFL Lines Lab — every published number is generated from data fetched and hashed by the repository's own pipeline. No betting return, payout or edge is claimed anywhere: the only market data available is a single un-timestamped line snapshot, and no player-prop market exists in any reachable source.
