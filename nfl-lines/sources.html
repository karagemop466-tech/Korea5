Injury mirror audit

Columns present: season, week, team, full_name, report_status. What was not verified

  • 2026 player-level totals from the archive (no 2026 week-level player file is reachable).
  • historical betting prices (no official source exists at all).
  • practice participation and injury severity in the 2025 mirror (columns absent).

Citation policy

  • A value that comes from a mirror is labelled MIRROR and links the mirror plus the upstream release.
  • A value that comes from nfl.com is labelled OFFICIAL and links the exact page.
  • A value that cannot be confirmed is blank and listed on the Flagged for Review page.
  • Market prices are labelled NOT OFFICIAL - no league source exists for them.
