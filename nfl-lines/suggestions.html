If you only act on three

  1. SG04 — score on the interval, not the ROI. Free, reversible, and it is the difference between a leaderboard that rewards skill and one that rewards variance.
  2. SG05 — freeze a prospective trial. No strategy can ever be promoted without it, so the competition cannot start until it happens.
  3. SG01 — decide pre-market or post-market lines. This changes what the fair line even means, and everything downstream depends on the answer.
