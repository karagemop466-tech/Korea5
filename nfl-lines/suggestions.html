Suggestions — review before implementing

A review queue, not a wish list. Each suggestion states the improvement to decision making, why it matters, the cheapest test that would show whether it works, its cost, and whether it is already implemented here or still proposed. Nothing in this list has been implemented on the owner's behalf beyond the items marked implemented, which are composition and layout choices inside this new section.

Recommended reading order for a decision

  1. Questions — is a decision even possible? Q1 and Q2 gate everything else.
  2. Data audit — do the inputs exist and do two publishers agree?
  3. Leaderboard — what survives deflation, with what sample size?
  4. Signals — are the inputs distinct, or the same input twice?
  5. Parlays — if combining, is the correlation measured or assumed?
  6. Flagged — what is blank, contradictory or stale?

What I would do first, if this were my decision

  1. S3 — start capturing timestamped market snapshots. Nothing else converts this from a research lab into a line-making system.
  2. S4 — add the inactive list, so the injury index becomes a fact rather than a proxy.
  3. S8 — run the paper trial before spending anything on rewards.
  4. S2 and S7 — tighten the statistics before adding more strategies. More strategies on the same data is how a lab manufactures a winner.

NFL Lines Lab — every published number is generated from data fetched and hashed by the repository's own pipeline. No betting return, payout or edge is claimed anywhere: the only market data available is a single un-timestamped line snapshot, and no player-prop market exists in any reachable source.
