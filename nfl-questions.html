Summary of what we have vs. do not have

We HAVE (official)

  • NFL schedule structure (18 weeks, kickoff anchor, Thanksgiving, Super Bowl window)
  • Weekly injury designations: Out / Doubtful / Questionable
  • Practice participation: Full / Limited / Did Not Participate
  • 2026 season fixtures and kickoff times

Sources: nfl.com/schedules, nfl.com/injuries

We DO NOT have (not official)

  • Official betting lines (moneyline / spread / total)
  • Official player-prop prices
  • Official correlation matrix
  • Official depth-chart / backup replacements
  • Validated strategy edge, win rate or rewards
  • 2027–2029 NFL schedules

All blank and flagged unverified; never inferred.

Suggestions to discover missing information

  • Subscribe to a licensed odds data feed for opening/closing lines and prop prices; treat it as a model input, not an official source, and flag every value unverified.
  • Parse each club's official roster/depth page weekly and cross-reference with the NFL injury report to build the replacement-by-string register.
  • Back-test candidate strategies against settled 2026 games using recorded lines; publish edge/win rate only after full-season validation.
  • Maintain a correlation register updated from the odds feed; disclose inputs before any parlay or prop combo enters the leaderboard.
