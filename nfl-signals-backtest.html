How the model is fitted

  • Opponent-adjusted ratings over 132 scored games; margin spread 11.26 points; home-field -0.52 points, estimated from the same window.
  • Published shrink factor λ = 0.80 on the rating gap, chosen on 15 validation games at log loss 0.4084. chosen inside the training window only (see choose_shrinkage) Weights by λ — {"0.2": 0.0239, "0.35": 0.0593, "0.5": 0.128, "0.7": 0.2729, "1.0": 0.5159}; argmin was λ 1.00; loss by λ — {"0.2": 0.61331, "0.35": 0.55256, "0.5": 0.50131, "0.7": 0.45084, "1.0": 0.40839}; the spread between the best and worst λ is 0.2049 nats, which is how little these 15 validation games decide.
  • Ties are excluded from log loss rather than scored as errors, and each ledger row prints the size of the window it was forecast from, so a forecast built on three games cannot hide among ones built on ninety.
  • The published forecast is an average over the shrink grid, weighted by exp(−n·Δlog loss), rather than one λ picked by argmin: choosing between 0.2 and 1.0 on fifteen validation games is a coin flip dressed as a decision, so λ that the data cannot separate share the answer, and the weights are printed below.
  • The margin spread is fitted against the shrunk forecast, not a separate unshrunk one; measuring them independently is how a model ends up confident and flat at the same time.

Not computable with what exists here

  • player-prop pair correlation — no player-level data in this repository
  • bookmaker parlay pricing correlation — no odds archive in this repository
  • weather effect on totals — no archived weather observation with a pre-game timestamp
  • referee-crew effect — no official per-crew split captured
  • bye-week / rest effect — the by-week payload carries no bye list
  • depth-chart tier (2nd/3rd/4th string) — nfl.com publishes no depth chart (probed: /depth-charts/ and the club depth-chart page both return 404), so a backup's quality cannot be verified from an official source

Player-prop combinations

No player-level season statistics and no player-prop prices are captured by tools/fetch_nfl_lab.py; nfl.com's player-statistics page renders client-side, and no prop price feed exists in this repository.

What would unblock it:

  • player snap / opportunity counts with a point-in-time timestamp
  • the offered prop line and its timestamp, per book
  • game-day inactives (official) to condition participation on

The method, ready to run the day the data exists:

  • forecast the player's full distribution, not a point estimate
  • price each leg off that distribution, then price the combo off the JOINT distribution
  • estimate pairwise association from settled joint outcomes only, and publish n with it
  • refuse a combo price when the joint sample does not exist
