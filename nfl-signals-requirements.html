Two gaps decide what can never be claimed here. There is no market-price archive, so no edge, ROI or closing-line statement is possible (R11/R12/R20); and there is no dated availability archive, so the injury adjustment cannot be judged historically (R9). Both are written as gates that fail, not as prose caveats.
