How to raise one

  • Add an entry to data/nfl-signals/suggestions.json with id, suggestion, raisedBy, status, decision, evidence and seenOn, then run python3 scripts/build_signals.py. The tests require a real reason and a place where the outcome is visible.
  • A suggestion that would publish a number the data cannot support is answered with the measurement that can be made: SG-05 asked for ROI and got a paired log-loss difference with a bootstrap interval instead.
  • Decisions recorded here are this build's reading of the brief; an owner can reverse any of them and the pages follow the file.

Framework, not just numbers. The research questions, gate design and reward proposal this build implements are written up in the NFL Lines & Strategy Lab. Requirement statuses live on the requirement ledger, and every decision taken about a suggestion is on the review board.

Written record. Executive summary covers the outcome and what would change it; method review covers what a reader must not conclude from these numbers, and the decisions that need an owner rather than another commit.
