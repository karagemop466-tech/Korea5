How this lab differs from the other NFL efforts in this repository. Several independent NFL pages have landed here — an intel desk, an analytics page, a paper skill lab, and the strategy proposal this build implements. This lab's difference is not vocabulary. Every row is bound to the official nfl.com page it came from; a score is attached to a game only when it sits inside that game's own link and both club nicknames agree; every modelled quantity is scored walk-forward and published with its standard error; and the gates that fail are printed as failures. Where an input does not exist — market prices, a dated availability archive, a depth chart — the page states the gap instead of estimating across it.

Where this goes next

  • The forward ledger settles itself once this runs after each game week; six settled slates is what turns the table above from provisional into evidence.
  • A dated availability archive is the single highest-value missing input: today the injury data is one snapshot, so the availability strategy fails its own leakage gate deliberately, and can only be judged prospectively.
  • Player-prop combinations stay uncomputed until player-level statistics and prop prices exist; the method is written into the engine so it runs the day the data arrives.
  • No official depth-chart page exists, so replacement quality is reported as a gap instead of being estimated from contract years or snap counts.

Reproduce: gh workflow run nfl-lab.yml for the capture (the sandbox has no outbound HTTP), then python3 scripts/signal_lab.py and python3 scripts/build_signals.py offline from the committed JSON. Machine-readable output: data/nfl-signals/signals.json.
