  • A trained, calibrated line-origination model and market-implied calibration (Q7).
  • Empirical correlation values computed from official play-level data (Q4).
  • Validated strategy performance; no strategy has passed the validation gates yet (Q5, FL5).
  • A historical injury/roster archive sufficient for back-testing (Q8).
  • Full per-market player-prop grading rules (Q9).

Method summary

Strategies must pass configurable validation gates (out-of-sample, no look-ahead, edge vs market, selectivity, significance). Correlation is estimated from play-level data and applied when combining legs. Luck is removed by out-of-sample testing, ranking on edge vs market / closing-line value, selectivity requirements and risk-adjusted returns. Lines are model-originated and clearly labelled.
