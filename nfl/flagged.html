Irregularities found and fixed during this session

  1. A wrong derivation was caught and corrected. The first pass computed points-per-game from the official team scoring table, which publishes touchdowns rather than points, producing a league mean of 12.97 points per game. The headers were read, the error identified, and points are now computed from official final scores (league mean 21.81 for 2026). The buggy figure is not published anywhere.
  2. A season blend was caught and corrected. The first pass pooled 2025 and 2026 finals into one scoring rate, which would have described "28 games" per club without saying it spanned two seasons. Rates are now current-season only, with the prior season kept separately and labelled.
  3. Houston's depth chart is an image. The parser returned nothing, and rather than substitute a guess the club is listed as unavailable with the reason.
  4. Green Bay's chart uses a different table layout. A second parser was added and verified against the live page; the Packers are now included, with jersey numbers stripped from names.
