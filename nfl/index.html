The honest state of the system in six lines

  1. Data is real and sourced. 185 completed 2026 games, 272 from 2025, 3,117 official injury rows for the current report week, 1,762 depth-chart entries across 31 clubs, and 7 official team stat tables. Each is fetched by CI and stamped.
  2. Prices do not exist. nfl.com publishes no odds. With no price, no bet can be settled and no return can be computed. Any page claiming a return would be inventing it.
  3. So nothing is certified. 453 official finals are enough to test the machinery, not enough to certify a strategy. The shuffle test on a home-field prior returns p = 1.000 — i.e. indistinguishable from a coin.
  4. Luck is separated from skill by construction. The gate suite permutes outcomes, so a strategy that only looks good because outcomes were kind scores no better than its shuffled twin.
  5. Injuries and depth are tracked as two separate facts. The official injury report gives status; the club depth chart gives who is next. A replacement is named only when the club's own chart names him.
  6. Every open decision is on one page. 18 questions, each with what we have, what we do not have, and the official source that would close it.

What the official data already says

Home advantage is a coin at this sample

Across 453 decided games in 2025 and 2026, the home team won 232 times (51.2%, 90% interval 47.4–55.1%).

The interval spans 50%, so a home-field "edge" is not demonstrated here. That is the correct reading, and it is exactly why the skill-vs-luck gate exists. official standings
www.nfl.com

Rest days are exact; travel is not

32 team-games in 2026 come off 4 or fewer rest days (minimum 4 days). Rest is computed from official kickoff dates, so it is exact. Travel distance is not official and is not included.

What we do not have — stated plainly

No prices. Not a single spread, moneyline, total or prop line exists in this system, because the official NFL pages publish none. Consequence: no ROI, no closing-line value, no claim of outperforming a price, no certified strategy.

No snap counts. Who actually played is not published as machine-readable data on the pages probed. Consequence: "the 2nd string had to start" is a depth-chart inference, labelled as such, never a measurement.

One club publishes its depth chart as an image. Houston's chart is a picture, so it is blank here rather than guessed. Consequence: 31 of 32 clubs have depth data.
