This is a decision-support research competition, not a wagering service. Rewards are illustrative of the design; no real-money wagering is performed by this tool. Betting carries risk.

Ranking metricRisk-adjusted, luck-adjusted correlation-adjusted edge vs market (closing-line value), selectivity-penalised. Raw hit rate is not the primary rank.
EligibilityA strategy is eligible only if it has passed every validation gate (M1) and meets the minimum out-of-sample sample size.
Tie-break1) correlation-adjusted edge vs market; 2) selectivity; 3) fewer bets (higher confidence); 4) alphabetical strategy name.
Evaluation windowRolling, out-of-sample only; no in-sample ranking is published.
