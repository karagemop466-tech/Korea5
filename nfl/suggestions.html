S03 Own lines Publish a fair price (no margin) plus an offered price at a stated hold, and label both, rather than publishing one ambiguous number.
why / options / risk
Why: A single number is unusable: you cannot tell if it is a probability, a price, or a price with juice. Separating fair from offered makes the model auditable.
Options: A: fair + offered (chosen default). B: fair only. C: offered only.
Risk: If the hold is not stated per market, the offered price is still ambiguous — hence the hold column.
Needs: Q02 must be answered by the owner to replace the default hold.

S04 Own lines Never show a model number without the sample it came from and the uncertainty band.
why / options / risk
Why: A point estimate invites overconfidence. With 8–10 games of official data, the band is wide and that is the honest finding.
Options: A: value + n + interval (chosen). B: value only.
Risk: Wide bands look weak; they are accurate.
Needs: Official per-game data (ingested).

S05 Skill vs luck Run a shuffle test that destroys any real edge, and only call a strategy skill-based when its observed score beats the shuffled distribution.
why / options / risk
Why: This is the direct operational answer to 'remove luck from the equation': if the same strategy applied to randomised outcomes scores as well, the result was luck.
Options: A: shuffle test + minimum sample (chosen). B: raw win rate.
Risk: A shuffle test controls for outcome randomness but not for data snooping across many strategies; that is why G7 (multiple testing) is a separate gate.
Needs: Settled outcomes (official final scores).
 accepted-built G3 Skill-vs-luck Monte-Carlo shuffle test, 2000 draws, printed p-value official
www.nfl.com

S08 Correlation Never multiply leg probabilities for a parlay. Publish the empirical joint probability, the independence product, and the difference.
why / options / risk
Why: The independence product is wrong whenever legs share a game or a player, and the size of the error is exactly what a parlay needs to price.
Options: A: empirical joint vs independent product, difference shown (chosen). B: independent product only (rejected).
Risk: Joint probabilities from few games are unstable; suppressed below n=8 shared games.
Needs: Q08, Q09.

The decision rules this register is held to

  1. A suggestion may not contradict a verified official value. If it would, the row links the official source it would contradict and stays 'awaiting review'.
  2. Anything requiring a price, a reward, or a person's judgement is capped at 'accepted-proposed-default' until the owner decides.
  3. No suggestion may introduce a number that is not either (a) computed from official ingested data, or (b) explicitly labelled MODEL/ESTIMATE with its method printed.

Where the individual decisions live

The questions that block specific suggestions are tracked one per row on Questions & gaps. The thresholds a strategy must clear are on Validation gates. The reason no suggestion can yet claim a betting edge is on Lines & market.
