Strategies &amp; competition

Ten pre-registered strategy hypotheses across single bets, parlays and player-prop combos, each with its inputs, its blocking gate and whether it is testable today.

Zero of these 10 strategies is certified. The registry exists so the comparison is pre-registered: the hypotheses, the inputs and the blocking gate are written down before any result is seen. That is what stops a leaderboard from being a search for the luckiest strategy.

How a strategy is scored

Primary
Brier score and log loss against the realised outcome (lower is better); both are proper scoring rules and cannot be gamed by hedging.
Secondary
Expected calibration error over probability deciles.
ROI only if
A stored, timestamped two-way price exists for every settled bet. Otherwise ROI is withheld, not estimated.
CLV only if
A stored closing price exists. CLV is the fastest skill signal but needs the price ledger (S20).

Never used as a ranking key:

  • Raw win rate as a ranking key
  • Longest winning streak
  • Units won without a stated stake rule
  • Any back-test figure that used information published after the decision cutoff
