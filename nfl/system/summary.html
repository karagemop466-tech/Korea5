Executive summary

What was asked. Originate NFL lines, generate useful signals, run a leaderboard competition with rewards for top strategies across single bets, parlays and player-prop combos, pass every strategy through validation gates, determine correlation values, remove luck from the equation, and track injuries and starter/backup deficiencies on both sides of the ball — with no manual input, no hallucination, and an official source for every value.

What was built. An ingest pipeline that runs in CI and pulls seven official datasets (scores, injury report, 32 club depth charts, seven team-stat tables, player-stat leaders, rosters, and the official schedule already in the repository); an analysis engine that computes rest days, scoring rates, de-vigged prices, correlations with bootstrap intervals, calibration and shuffle tests; a nine-gate validation suite; and this site, which publishes the results and the gaps with the same prominence.

What it proves. Machinery, not edge. With 453 decided games the home-field prior is statistically indistinguishable from a coin (shuffle p = 1.000), and with no stored prices not one strategy can be settled. The leaderboard therefore ranks nobody, and says so. That is the honest output, and it is a real result: it tells you exactly what the next decision must be.

What it cannot prove — and why that is not a failure of effort. A line requires a price; a price requires a market feed; the NFL does not publish one. Correlation between teammates requires per-game player logs joined by week, which the ingested leader boards are not. Snap counts, travel and weather are not machine-readable on the official pages probed. Each of these is named, sourced and costed on the Questions page rather than papered over.

The one decision that unblocks the most. Decide the price source (Q01). Everything downstream — ROI, closing-line value, parlay settlement, reward allocation, certification — is blocked on it. The second is the reward policy (Q04); the third is the reviewer for this register (Q17).
