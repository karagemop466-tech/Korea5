Other live sport on Bay Area AM/FM radio

You asked us to look for every other sport carried live on Bay Area radio and to treat it as high priority. This page is that search: what we found, the station it airs on, the official source for each claim, and what we deliberately did not guess.

Important: these are not, by default, part of your "free" definition. The brief defines a free minute as one with no MLB, no NFL, no Stanford, no Cal and no Westwood One national NFL broadcast. That is a closed list, so the rows on this page are held in their own group and do not block free time by default. The vacation page shows both answers side by side so you can see exactly what changes if you switch them on. If you want them to block, say so and it is a one-line change.

Irregularities and limits on this page

  • nba.com/warriors/schedule prints tip times without a timezone label. They are read as Pacific (team-local); the club's official season PDF uses the same numbers, which is the cross-check used.
  • SOURCE CONFLICT — 2026-11-18 vs 2026-11-17 at Dallas: the live schedule page says Wed Nov 18, 6:30 PM; the club's September PDF places DAL at Tue Nov 17, 6:30P. The live page's date is used and the conflict is flagged.
  • SOURCE CONFLICT — 2026-11-27 vs 2026-11-28 at Portland: the live schedule page says Fri Nov 27, 6:30 PM; the PDF places POR at Sat Nov 28, 6:30P. The live page's date is used and the conflict is flagged.
  • UNRESOLVED — one Warriors game against Dallas in the week of 2027-02-21 appears on the official PDF with no readable column association. It is omitted rather than assigned to a guessed date; the week 2027-02-21..2027-02-27 should be treated as uncertain.
  • The official PDF prints 'CUP GAMES QUARTERFINALS / SEMIFINALS / CHAMPIONSHIP' placeholders (Dec 4-11, 2026). Those are not confirmed Warriors games and are not ingested.
  • The official San Jose Sharks flagship confirmation found is an NHL.com/Sharks article dated 2019. No newer official statement was located this session; it is flagged for re-verification.
  • San Jose Sharks game rows were NOT ingested: nhl.com/sharks/schedule renders one month at a time, so a full-season feed is outside this session's retrieval scope.
  • No official average NBA game length was confirmed from an official source. The 150-minute busy block is an unverified planning estimate and is published on the Flagged page as such.
