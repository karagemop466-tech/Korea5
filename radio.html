What is actually on the radio in San Francisco

The calendar is only useful if the games are reachable with a plain AM/FM radio. This page records which stations are documented by the teams themselves, and which claims we could not confirm.

Practical answer for 94122. KNBR (680 AM / 104.5 FM) is the safe default: it carries the Giants, the 49ers and Stanford. Cal is on KSFO 810 AM. That set covers every high-priority team on this site except the Athletics, whose 2026 flagship we could not verify — so A's games are blocked in the calendar (they are high priority) even though their station is unconfirmed.

Westwood One national NFL windows

Timing difference worth knowing. On every game carried by both sources, Westwood One's published start is 15–75 minutes earlier than the NFL's official kickoff — it is the start of the network's broadcast, not the snap. The planner therefore gives each game a single row: the radio goes busy at the earlier (Westwood One) time, and the kickoff time from the NFL schedule is noted on the row.
