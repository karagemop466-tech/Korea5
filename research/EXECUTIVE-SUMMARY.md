**Review date: 2026-11-03 · San Francisco 94122 · America/Los_Angeles**

## Decision

**No 7-, 14- or 21-day conflict-free vacation has been certified for 2027, 2028 or 2029.**
This is not a finding that such vacations are impossible. Complete official schedules,
field-level validation and station-specific terrestrial carriage evidence are missing.
It is not responsible to name an exact best week as a verified fact.

The inherited model ranks January/February gaps relatively well. Those are only planning
candidates: NFL postseason, special events, MLB spring training and additional Westwood
One sports can invalidate them. Consult the provisional [rankings](../extra/vacation.html).
No exact candidate dates are recommended as verified booking windows.

## Verification performed this session

Reviewed the existing generators, calendars, future rankings and historical reports.
Fixed future coverage flags so no future vacation is marked fully verified-free. Published
a prominent certainty correction on every site page and retained historical records for
review. Fixed the root calendar's Today control to use the current Pacific date and
changed visible gaps to model-gap language. TBD rows in the combined calendar suppress
numeric free hours and gap suggestions rather than silently ignoring the uncertainty.

Local tests validate links, data structure, source-link presence and certainty policy.
They do **not** verify every fact against a freshly retrieved official source. Existing
source-token checks can use snapshots and match unrelated tokens; they cannot certify
exhaustive schedules. The sandbox cannot directly reach official sports hosts. Historical
verification timestamps are preserved rather than presented as today's verification.

Pacific: PST in winter, PDT in summer. Halftime is included within a game, not free time.
