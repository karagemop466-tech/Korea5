How the verification works

  1. MLB. Games are pulled from the league's own StatsAPI. A CI job re-queries six sample dates per season and compares the game IDs with the stored feed — any drift fails the check.
  2. 49ers, Stanford, Cal, Westwood One. Each curated row carries verifyTokens (the date, opponent and time as printed by the official page). CI re-downloads the page and asserts every token is still present.
  3. Snapshots. A few official sites render their schedule client-side. For those, the row is checked against the committed text snapshot taken from the same URL (research/text/) and the report records which method was used.
  4. Failures are published, not hidden. Anything that fails appears on Flagged for review with its source link.
