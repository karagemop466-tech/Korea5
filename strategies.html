Removing luck from the equation

  • Grade on closing-line value (CLV) as well as outcomes: a strategy that beats the close repeatedly has skill even in a losing month (needs Q4/Q17 resolved).
  • Shrink ROI toward the mean: shrunkROI = (n*ROI + k*0)/(n+k) with k = 25; publish n beside every score.
  • Bootstrap 95% CI (10,000 resamples of the pick ledger); rewards require the interval to clear zero.
  • Deflate parlays: expected variance of a parlay grows superlinearly; count a parlay as its legs for sample-size purposes.
  • Separate 'process score' (did the strategy follow its own rules?) from 'outcome score' — process errors invalidate the week regardless of result.
  • Report luck-adjusted rank = shrunkROI rank + CLV rank blend (60/40), never raw ROI alone.

Method references and formulas live in Leaderboard scoring; the empirical job that replaces the estimates is S5.
