What this lab is

The lab originates its own NFL numbers from official league data rather than copying a bookmaker's line. It currently holds 137 verified game results from the 2026 season, covering Weeks 1 to 10, plus the official standings and the Week 10 official injury report. Everything below is computed from those rows by strategy-strategy-lab/pipeline.py, and every row links back to the official page it came from.

It deliberately does not claim a betting edge. One model clears the significance gate, one fails, one fails on significance, totals fail outright, and props and parlays are blocked on missing data. That is the honest state of the work.

Executive summary - the important details

  1. The naive model is worse than doing nothing. Unshrunk scoring rates produce a margin MAE of 10.55 against 10.21 for a constant zero forecast. It fails gate G3 outright and is kept only as a labelled control.
  2. Shrinking team rates toward the league mean is what creates the signal. Shrunk variant: MAE 9.61 vs 10.21, a paired improvement of 0.599 points per game (t = 2.274, one-sided p = 0.0115, bootstrap 90% CI 0.166 to 1.035). This is the only strategy that clears gate G3.
  3. Totals show no skill in any variant. The best total MAE is 10.91 against a constant baseline of 10.96. Gate G6 fails. Do not trade totals from this model.
  4. Momentum is not a signal in this data. The lag-1 autocorrelation of a team's own game margin is r = 0.086 across 242 team-week pairs. Last week's result carries essentially no information about this week.
  5. Same-game parlay legs are not independent. Home points vs game total correlates r = 0.681 and away points vs game total r = 0.782, because both legs share a team's scoring. Multiplying marginals is invalid.
  6. There is no official depth chart, so backup string order cannot be known today. The NFL.com team depth-chart URL returns HTTP 404 and the official roster lists position and ACT status only. The 2nd/3rd/4th-string requirement is blocked, not implemented with a guess.
  7. No market price exists anywhere in this lab. Every spread, total, moneyline and prop cell is blank and marked unverified. No official NFL source publishes betting prices, so closing-line value and ROI cannot yet be computed at all.
  8. Home-field advantage is effectively zero this season. Across 137 games the mean home margin is -0.02 points and home teams are 67-69-1. On true home fields only the mean is 0.47 points. Whether that persists is unknown - flag F08.
