What the backtest actually did

Chronological walk-forward. For each test week from Week 4 onwards, the model is fitted using only games from strictly earlier weeks, including the shrinkage weight, which is chosen by leave-one-out error inside that training fold. The test week is then predicted and scored. No test-week information ever reaches the fit, which is what gate G4 checks. Scoring uses mean absolute error on the margin against two baselines (a constant zero forecast and a constant equal to the training-set mean margin), and the Brier score against a constant 0.50 forecaster.
