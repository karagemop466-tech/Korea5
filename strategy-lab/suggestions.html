Suggestions

Improvements to decision making, each with explicit paths to choose between before anything is built.

Verification rule. Every row on this site links to the official page it came from. Where an official source has not published a value the cell is left blank and the row is listed on the Flagged for Review page. Nothing is inferred and nothing is filled in.

How to use this page

Each suggestion is a decision, not a task. Every row offers two or three explicit paths with their upsides and downsides, and one path is marked recommended. Nothing is implemented until a path is chosen and the row is moved out of proposed. To record a decision, change the status field for that row in strategy-strategy-lab/content.py and rebuild; the page regenerates from it.

Status vocabulary: proposed awaiting a decision · approved path chosen, ready to implement · deferred deliberately parked · rejected considered and declined · done implemented.
