Suggestions — review before implementing

Nothing on this page is implemented until it is accepted. This is the decision-making queue: read, choose the best path, mark the status. Statuses: proposed → accepted / rejected / needs-info → implemented.

Review workflow

  1. 1. Anyone adds a suggestion with rationale, expected impact and affected files/pages.
  2. 2. The reviewer (site owner) marks each suggestion: accepted / rejected / needs-info / implemented. Nothing is implemented before it is accepted.
  3. 3. Accepted suggestions are ordered by the priority column and executed in a separate change with its own verification pass.
  4. 4. Implemented suggestions keep their ID and gain a link to the change that implemented them.
  5. 5. Rejected suggestions stay on the page with the rejection reason, so decisions are auditable.

Decision owner: site owner · cadence: weekly during the NFL season; monthly otherwise · rule: A suggestion may only move to accepted when its linked questions (if any) are answered or explicitly waived.
