Vacation windows, 2027–2029

Ranked by how much of the trip is genuinely free. Two lenses: strict (days with zero qualifying games) and lightest (the fewest hours of live sport per day).

There is no 7-day, 14-day or 21-day window in 2027–2029 with zero games. The longest fully free runs are 6 days in 2027, 6 days in 2028 and 6 days in 2029. MLB plays a full slate almost every day from late February to early October, and NFL/college football covers the winter weekends.

The practical answer

Take the mid-February gap. Each year the calendar has one clean pocket: after the Super Bowl and before the first Spring Training game. In 2027 that is Feb 8–13 (six nights, Mon–Sat). In 2028 and 2029 the same structure repeats (see the year pages below). It is the only stretch in the three years where you can go away and miss nothing at all.
If the trip must be longer than a week, the honest trade-off is: accept one NFL Sunday (about 3 hours of radio) or the Pro Bowl, and you can stretch the February window to 12–14 days with only a handful of busy hours — that is the best 14-day score in all three years. In-season months (April–September) score badly on the strict test because MLB plays daily, but the actual listening burden is only about three hours a night.
