Vacation windows, 2027–2029

Two lenses, both computed from the event feed. Radio counts only games that are actually broadcast (Giants, Athletics, 49ers, Stanford, Cal, Westwood One national, and the Pro Bowl Games, which Westwood One carried in 2026). All games also counts every other MLB and NFL row in the scope. In the January–February windows the two lenses agree.

No 7-night window is free of every listed game in 2027, 2028 or 2029. The longest free run is 6 calendar days in 2027, 6 in 2028 and 6 in 2029. In 2027 the two 6-night runs are Jan 25–30 and Feb 1–6. The 2027 Pro Bowl date is not published, so it is the one assumption that moves the answer (scenario table below).

No 7-, 14- or 21-day window is fully free in any year. The ranked lists below show the windows with the fewest busy days.

Why not the summer? MLB plays a slate almost every day from late February to early October, and your scope counts every MLB game, not just the Giants and Athletics. So April–September is busy on nearly every day by definition. The winter gap is the only place a multi-week trip fits.
